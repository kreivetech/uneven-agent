# Objection-Handling Patterns (1st Person, Plain Text)

Keep every reply in 1st person singular, without em-dashes (`—`), without `https://` links or backticks, and without technical jargon or self-explanations. Match the prospect's language.

1. **Data privacy / AI exposure:**
   - *Concern:* How do we know our data is not sent to external AI providers?
   - *Pattern:* Clarify that the AI only sees the user's request to prepare the action/query, and execution happens only after team review inside the company's own environment. No customer records leave the perimeter.

2. **Friction or risk to evaluate:**
   - *Concern:* Do we have to connect our production data just to test it?
   - *Pattern:* Offer a live walkthrough first so the team can see the full workflow before connecting any real environment, mentioning the website in plain text.

3. **Proprietary engineering & security:**
   - *Concern:* Is this built in-house or wrapped around off-the-shelf tools?
   - *Pattern:* State directly that the engineering base was built from scratch with security validation throughout the lifecycle, without listing libraries or frameworks.

4. **Support & structure:**
   - *Concern:* How does support work if something breaks?
   - *Pattern:* Emphasize direct contact with the person responsible for engineering and support, without intermediaries.

5. **Early-stage / production references:**
   - *Concern:* Who is already using this in production?
   - *Pattern:* Be transparent about onboarding initial corporate pilots and offer a practical demonstration to validate security and workflow before any commitment.

6. **Pricing & commercial model:**
   - *Concern:* How does pricing work?
   - *Pattern:* Present a predictable monthly model by team tier or usage without surprises, while prioritizing a quick demo to confirm fit first.
